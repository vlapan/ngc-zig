### CIDR Algorithm Problem
- Original formula `clz(start)` gives wrong prefix
- For IP `1.0.0.0` (start=16777216): `@clz(16777216) = 8`, prefix=24 (WRONG)
- Should be `@clz(16777215)` for remaining space = prefix
- Correct formula: `prefix = 32 - ctz(start)` or `prefix = 32 - clz(remaining)`
