## Perf Convergence Redesign (final)

Fixed the convergence methodology to produce deterministic, budget-free results.

### What was wrong

1. **`near_exact` used exact equality** (`elapsed_ns == running_min`). With ns-precise timer and 5ms batches (~5,000,000ns), exact matches are statistically impossible — expected <1 hit in 400 batches. Convergence was always hitting BUDGET.

2. **Unidirectional drift used consecutive-counter** (5 negative SMA deltas → reset). Random SMA oscillation generates 5-consecutive-same-direction chains by chance (50% per step), resetting the stall counter ~12 times per 400 batches. Stall never reached 10.

3. **NEAR_MIN_WINDOW=1.005 (0.5%)** was too tight around the absolute minimum. Only 1-2.5% of batches fall within 0.5% of min (debug confirmed: near_min=2-5 at 200 batches). NEAR_MIN_REQUIRED=10 was unreachable.

4. **MAX_BUDGET_MS=2000** was compensating for non-convergence. With proper criteria, benchmarks converge in 170-250ms.

### What was fixed

| Parameter | Before | After | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-------|-----------|
| `near_exact` criterion | `== running_min` (3 required) | removed | Impossible with ns timer |
| `NEAR_MIN_WINDOW` | 1.005 | 1.01 | Catches ~8% of batches within 1% of min |
| `NEAR_MIN_REQUIRED` | 10 | 5 | Achievable: 200×8% = 16 expected (set to 5 for robustness) |
| Drift check | 5 consecutive negative deltas | T75 SMA with ±0.1% stability | Random oscillation stays within threshold |
| `MAX_BUDGET_MS` | 2000 | 1000 | Convergence at 170-260ms |

### Results

All 5 benchmarks converge without BUDGET at 44-269 batches (44-270ms). Run-to-run variance depends on noise — tighter thresholds (PCTL_STALL=20, MIN_AFTER_MIN=20, NEAR_MIN_REQUIRED=5) produce higher batch counts for SW-PRF-001 which has bimodal timing.
