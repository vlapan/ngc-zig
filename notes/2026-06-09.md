## Session 3: Perf Redesign — Min measurement, T50 convergence

### What changed
Methodology went through P25 → P75 → T75 → T50 → Min-with-T50-convergence. Final approach:

- **Measurement**: Min (absolute minimum batch time)
- **Convergence**: T50 SMA ±1% for 5 consecutive batches + min unchanged for 5 batches
- **No frequency correction**: calibration loop (ALU) over-corrects memory-bound benchmarks (SWAR). Preheat + warmup keep frequency stable enough. Correction was adding ±3-7% noise.
- **Display columns**: Min | T50 | Max | Noise | Batches | Delta

### Key findings
- P25: ±2-4% across runs — robust to right-tail interference, but single lucky batch pulls it down
- P75: ±3-7% — WORSE, captures right tail where interference lives
- T50 (middle 50% trimmed mean): ±2-5%, better than P75 but still affected by distribution-wide shifts
- **Min (no correction)**: ±0.05-0.3% within-session, best approach
- Frequency correction adds noise because calibration (ALU-bound integer add) doesn't scale the same as benchmarks (memory-bound SWAR, mixed CIDR)
- Within-session stability is what matters: baseline is re-recorded per session
- Across-session variation (±2-5%) is accepted — developer re-records baseline
